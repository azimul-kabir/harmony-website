YOUR COLLECTION, CONNECTED

From playlist to
your music library.

Harmony is the bridge between the music you discover and the library you manage on your own server.

01 / PLAYLIST SOURCES

Pick up where your playlists are.

Save Spotify and public YouTube Music playlists as Sources. Sync on demand or set an automatic schedule; playlist order is preserved.

02 / TRACK DOWNLOADS

Bring missing tracks home.

Download tracks, albums, and playlists with a background queue, live progress, retries, and concurrent workers. Harmony validates Spotify matches before importing them.

03 / YOUR LIBRARY

Find and manage every track.

Search indexed songs, albums, artists, genres, playlists, and identifiers. Edit metadata and artwork, import MP3, FLAC, M4A, Ogg, or Opus files, and check library health.

04 / MUSIC SERVERS

Your playlists, ready to play.

Export standard M3U playlists for Navidrome, Jellyfin, Plex, Kodi, VLC, and other compatible players. Harmony also syncs playlists directly to Navidrome.

THE DETAILS THAT MAKE IT YOURS

More than a playlist
downloader.

Maintain your collection with practical, careful tools—while keeping control over what gets changed or removed.

05PLAYLISTS

Sync playlists on your schedule.

Schedule a Spotify or public YouTube Music Source to sync hourly, every 6 or 12 hours, daily, or weekly. Harmony keeps playlist order, skips tracks that aren’t available, and queues missing music.

How playlist sync works ↗
06METADATA & ARTWORK

Edit tags. Choose your artwork.

Correct a song’s title, artist, album, genre, year, track, or disc. Search MusicBrainz for a better match and preview cover art before you explicitly save changes.

Explore metadata editing ↗
07LOCAL MUSIC

Import tracks you already have.

Bring in tracks downloaded with other tools or already on your device. Upload MP3, FLAC, M4A/MP4, Ogg, and Opus files, review their tags, and check for existing tracks before importing—without transcoding audio.

Read about local imports ↗
08DUPLICATE REVIEW

Spot duplicates. Stay in control.

Review likely duplicates grouped by match confidence. Harmony refreshes the comparison and asks you to confirm before any non-keeper files are deleted.

Learn about library health ↗
09LIBRARY MANAGEMENT

Take care of a whole collection.

Move, rename, refresh metadata or artwork, fetch album art, and export selected tracks. Check missing details and artwork from Library Health.

See library management tools ↗
10SETTINGS · OPERATIONS

Back up your library. Restore when needed.

Download a consistent SQLite database snapshot with cached artwork. Restore a backup to replace Harmony’s database and recover its archived artwork from Settings → Operations.

Read about backup and restore ↗

Product details reflect Harmony v3.1.0. Backups include the database and cached artwork; environment secrets and paths aren’t included.

Read the complete installation guide ↗
GETTING STARTEDbash

# Get the project and configure it
$ git clone https://github.com/azimul-kabir/harmony.git
$ cd harmony
$ cp .env.example .env

# Set WEB_AUTH_PASSWORD in .env, then start
$ docker compose up -d --build

Harmony’s web authentication is enabled by default. Set a long, unique WEB_AUTH_PASSWORD before you start.
